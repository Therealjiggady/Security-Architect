## 👤 Account Management

### Creating an Account
1. Click **"Sign Up"** in the top navigation
2. Fill out the registration form:
   - **Email Address:** Use a valid email you can access
   - **Full Name:** Your real name for personalization
   - **Password:** Create a strong, secure password
3. Click **"Create Account"**
4. You'll be automatically logged in and redirected to your dashboard

### Logging In
1. Click **"Login"** in the top navigation
2. Enter your **email** and **password**
3. Click **"Log In"**
4. You'll be redirected to your personalized homepage

### Account Security
- **Password Requirements:** Minimum 8 characters recommended
- **Secure Sessions:** You'll stay logged in until you log out manually
- **Data Protection:** Your personal information is encrypted and secure

Our AI-powered size recommender helps you find the perfect fit using your body measurements or quick inputs.

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues & Solutions

**Can't Log In**
- Verify your email and password are correct
- Check if Caps Lock is enabled
- Try resetting your password
- Clear your browser cache and cookies

**Cart Items Disappearing**
- Cart resets when you log out
- Add items to wishlist for permanent saving
- Items may be removed if stock becomes unavailable

**Chat Not Working**
- Ensure you're logged in
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Disable browser ad-blockers

**Size Recommender Issues**
- Enter accurate height and weight
- Try different input formats (imperial/metric)
- Clear and re-enter your measurements
- Contact support if problems persist

**Page Loading Slowly**
- Check your internet connection
- Clear browser cache
- Try a different browser
- Disable unnecessary browser extensions

**Payment Problems**
- Verify your payment information is correct
- Check if your card has sufficient funds
- Try a different payment method
- Contact our support team for assistance

## ❓ FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: Is my personal information secure?**
A: Yes, we use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data. Your payment information is processed securely and never stored on our servers.

**Q: Do I need to create an account to shop?**
A: While you can browse products without an account, you need to register to add items to your cart, use the chat feature, save wishlists, and make purchases.

**Q: How do I change my password?**
A: Go to "My Account" → "Profile Settings" → "Change Password". You'll need to enter your current password and choose a new one.

### Shopping Questions

**Q: How accurate is the size recommender?**
A: Our AI-powered size recommender provides suggestions based on your inputs and product specifications. Accuracy improves with more detailed measurements. We recommend also checking size charts and product reviews.

**Q: Can I modify my order after placing it?**
A: Contact our support team immediately via chat. We can often modify orders that haven't entered processing yet.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: We accept major credit cards, debit cards, and popular digital payment methods. Specific options are shown during checkout.

**Q: How do I track my order?**
A: Order tracking information is available in your account under "Order History". You'll also receive tracking updates via email.

### Technical Questions

**Q: Which browsers are supported?**
A: We support all modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. For the best experience, please keep your browser updated.

**Q: Is the website mobile-friendly?**
A: Yes! Our website is fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices, tablets, and desktop computers.

**Q: Why am I not receiving chat messages?**
A: Ensure you're connected to the internet and logged in. Try refreshing the page or switching chat rooms. If issues persist, contact support.

### Account Questions

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Contact our support team via chat, and they can help you with account deletion. Please note this action is permanent.

**Q: Can I have multiple accounts?**
A: We recommend using one account per person for the best experience and to avoid confusion with orders and wishlists.

**Q: I forgot my password, what do I do?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter your email address. We'll send you instructions to reset your password.
